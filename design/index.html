07

Rules to keep.

Keep the interface quiet.

Use the pulse as a brand mark, not a decoration on every card. Flat fills, one-pixel dividers, modest radii. Reserve the large acid field for a primary action or an occasional brand moment.

Keep data honest.

Never use a decorative pulse as a live ECG. Label bpm and individual limits. Show a dash for unavailable values and distinguish connecting, connected, lost, and offline.

Keep everyone legible.

Use names and initials alongside member colors. Pair warnings with text and shape. Support font scaling and scrolling rather than shrinking content to fit.

Keep motion purposeful.

Use short 120–240 ms transitions for direct feedback. No flashing pulse or continuously glowing logo. Respect reduced motion. Announce state changes, not every heart-rate sample.
